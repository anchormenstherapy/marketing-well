About the client

Dr. Darren Stolow is a Clinical Psychologist based in Montreal offering online counselling.

Services provided

Google Ads Setup Campaign Management Conversion Tracking

Overview

Dr. Stolow initially worked for another practice while building his private practice. He sought to expand without a physical office, focusing on remote sessions. After initial hesitation about Google Ads, we launched his campaign in June 2024.

Strategy

Targeting

We focused on people within a 15 km radius of Montreal. The landing pages emphasized that Dr. Stolow is a Montreal-based Registered Clinical Psychologist offering online sessions.

Adgroups & Keywords

Four adgroups — three target specific mental health concerns, one captures general therapy searches.

Tracking

We set up Google Analytics goals to track web form submissions and keyword performance, giving us a clear picture of which searches were driving real leads.
