Protect what you've built — and ensure your family keeps it

Estate tax planning isn't just paperwork. It's the difference between your legacy being preserved and your family paying more than they should. We make sure it's the former.

Overview

The cost of not planning is paid by the people you care about most

Most people don't think about estate taxes until it's too late. By then, the options are limited, the timelines are tight, and the people left to sort it out are already dealing with enough. That's not a plan — that's a problem.

At James & McIntyre, we help you get ahead of it. We work with you to estimate potential tax liabilities, structure your affairs to minimize the burden on your estate, and ensure your beneficiaries receive as much as possible — with as little stress as possible.

And if you're already administering an estate as an executor, we're here to take the complexity off your plate. From final tax returns to clearance certificates, we handle the details so you can focus on what matters.

"Estate planning isn't about what happens to your money when you're gone — it's about giving your family clarity, confidence, and one less thing to worry about during the hardest time of their lives."
