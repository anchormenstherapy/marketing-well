About This Location

Professional accountants on the Sunshine Coast

Hiring professional accountants is crucial for managing personal and business finances effectively. In Gibsons, our accountants will guide you through tax compliance, financial planning, and business strategies tailored to the unique needs of Sunshine Coast residents and business owners.

Our Gibsons office has been a trusted presence in the community for many years, led by Partner Linda Iverson, CPA, CGA, who is well known for her practical approach and long-standing client relationships. Whether you're a local business owner, a retiree managing your estate, or an individual who needs straightforward tax help — our team is here to assist.
