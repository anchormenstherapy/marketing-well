The right structure from day one saves you years of headaches

Incorporation, tax registration, bookkeeping — the decisions you make at the start determine what your business looks like for years. We make sure you get them right.

Overview

Starting a business is exciting — getting the accounting wrong isn't

Should you incorporate? Register for GST? Hire employees or contractors? Do the bookkeeping yourself or outsource it? How do you pay yourself to minimize taxes? What should you budget for? These aren't questions you should be guessing at.

At James & McIntyre, we help new business owners answer these questions before they become expensive mistakes. We set up the right legal structure, register you with the right authorities, and build the bookkeeping and reporting foundation your business needs to scale.

The answers will be unique to your business, your industry, and your personal situation. That's exactly why you need a CPA who takes the time to understand all three — not a one-size-fits-all template from the internet.

"Every decision you make in the first year of your business has tax consequences for years to come. We make sure you're making the right ones from day one."
