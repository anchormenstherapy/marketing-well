Our Practice

We provide personalized outpatient care in a warm, supportive environment. Our services include neurodivergent affirming care, individual therapy for adults and adolescents, and healing transgenerational trauma.

Our Focus

We help you embrace vulnerability, heal, and discover your inner strength. We listen with care and without judgment, tailoring treatment to your unique needs so you feel supported every step of the way.

Our Hope

That you find strength in your struggles, learn from your pain, and move toward the life you want. Through therapy, we'll support you in building trust in yourself and embracing new possibilities.

MACP Individual Therapy

$105 / 50 min

Our MACP counsellors are highly trained therapists completing the final step toward full licensure. While awaiting their Registered Clinical Counsellor (RCC) approval, they offer competent, compassionate care at a reduced rate. Once fully licensed, their rates will shift to the standard fee.
