After the accident

A car accident can affect more than your body. You might find yourself carrying stress, fear, or anxiety. Some people develop flashbacks, intrusive memories, or PTSD.

At Growing Minds, we provide specialized counselling for car accident recovery. We help you process the emotional impact, reduce stress, and regain confidence, so you can get back behind the wheel and retake control of your life.

Common symptoms

What you might be feeling.

  • Anxiety, panic, or fear around driving or being a passenger
  • PTSD, depression, or panic attacks
  • Nightmares, intrusive memories, or flashbacks
  • Difficult life transitions following the accident
  • Grief around injury or loss
  • Emotional irritability, numbness, or detachment
  • Difficulty concentrating, focusing, or feeling motivated

How ICBC funding works

Counselling at no cost to you.

If you were the driver or a passenger in a motor vehicle accident and have an open claim with ICBC, you may be eligible for counselling at no cost to you.

Most people receive 12 counselling sessions to be completed within 12 weeks of the accident. If you need more sessions after that, we send ICBC updates and apply for additional sessions on your behalf.

Getting started

What we’ll need from you.

To find out if Alisa is the right counsellor for you, book a complimentary 30-minute ICBC consultation. To set up direct billing, please have the following ready:

  • Date of birth
  • Date of the accident
  • ICBC claim number
  • Adjuster’s name

If it has been 12 weeks since your accident, we’ll also need a doctor’s note indicating your need for counselling connected with the accident.

Once we have this information, we apply for your funding through direct billing. In most cases you can start treatment within a week of contacting us.
