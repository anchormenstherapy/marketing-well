Coverage & billing

Therapy that may already be covered

Many clients pay little or nothing out of pocket. We bill these programs directly so you can focus on healing.

Crime Victim Assistance (CVAP)

Approved counsellors for the B.C. Crime Victim Assistance Program. If you're a victim, family member, or witness to a crime, your full counselling fee may be covered.

ICBC Direct Billing

Injured in a motor-vehicle accident? Our counsellors are trained in accident-related trauma and bill ICBC directly — no upfront cost to you.

EFAP / EAP

Many Employee & Family Assistance Programs let you choose your own counsellor. We work with most providers and submit receipts for reimbursement.
