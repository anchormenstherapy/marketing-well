Overview

Tax compliance is the floor, not the ceiling

Most accountants file your taxes. We plan them. The difference is the gap between what you owe and what you could owe — and for business owners with corporate structures, real estate holdings, or shareholder compensation questions, that gap can be significant.

At James & McIntyre, tax is not a once-a-year conversation. We work with you throughout the year to identify opportunities, anticipate changes, and structure your affairs in a way that minimizes your exposure and maximizes what you keep.

Our clients aren't looking for the cheapest tax filing. They're looking for a team that understands their business, knows the rules inside and out, and isn't afraid to pick up the phone and give them a straight answer when they need one.

"Whether the advice you need is for a specific problem, a unique situation, or 'it's complicated' — we've got the expertise to navigate it and the responsiveness to do it on your timeline."
