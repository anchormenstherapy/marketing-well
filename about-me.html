About Me

Hi, I'm Jordan Caron.

I started my career as a Golf Professional at Victoria Golf Club, but life had other plans. After losing both my brother and my father, I found myself drawn to something more meaningful and pursued digital marketing.

In 2012, I launched Meaningful Marketing and went on to help over 100 small businesses with SEO and Google Ads. Then, in 2018, I started working with therapists and witnessed something remarkable: how quickly the right marketing could fill their practices with clients who truly needed help.

By 2022, I made the decision to work exclusively with therapists and wellness practitioners. That decision became Marketing Well.

"I deeply understand the powerful impact your work can have on the lives of people suffering. I want to help you get your message out there so you can heal as many people as possible and grow a successful practice."

Off the Clock

Life on Salt Spring Island, BC

When I'm not helping therapists grow their practices, you'll find me on Salt Spring Island, BC — a small, nature-filled island off the coast of Vancouver Island. It's the kind of place that keeps you grounded.

I'm deeply interested in spirituality and personal growth, and I spend a lot of time exploring both. Music production and DJ'ing are longtime passions — there's something about creating music that balances out the analytical side of marketing.

I'm also an avid golfer (old habits die hard), a runner, and I love to travel whenever I get the chance. These interests keep me creative, curious, and energized for the work I do.
